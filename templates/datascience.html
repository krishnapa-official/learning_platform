About This Course

Data Science combines statistics, machine learning and data visualization to extract meaningful insights from raw data. It helps businesses optimize operations, understand customers, and predict future trends.

It answers important business questions like:

  • What do customers want?
  • How can we improve our services?
  • What will be the upcoming sales trend?
  • How much stock is needed for festivals?

You will learn Python, statistics, data wrangling, machine learning models, and data visualization tools like Matplotlib and Seaborn — everything you need to become a job-ready data scientist.
